To put your Vimeo service on the website

  1. Sign in to Vimeo and open the church's live event.
  2. Copy its public event link (or the Embed → Player only code).
  3. Open Pages CMS, then choose Watch Live (Vimeo).
  4. Paste the link, select Save, then test the Watch Live page.

This shortcut page does not hold passwords or edit your website itself. Pages CMS, Vimeo, and Cloudflare each require their normal sign-in when needed. If you have not uploaded the Vimeo website upgrade yet, the Watch Live page will not be available.
