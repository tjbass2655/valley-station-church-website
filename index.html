A Message of Welcome

More than a church.
A family in Christ.

At Valley Station Church of God, we believe the love of Jesus changes lives. Our desire is to make room for people to encounter God, build lasting friendships, and discover a meaningful place to serve.

We value heartfelt worship, sound Bible teaching, faithful prayer, and caring for one another. Whether you have attended church for years or are visiting for the first time, you are welcome here.

Pastor Timothy Bass
